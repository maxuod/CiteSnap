# Source and delivery notice

The seven original browser runtime modules were extracted from the owner's getaudit working tree on 2026-10-30. Their exact source hashes and base-commit context are recorded in `source-provenance.json`. The historical 0.1.0 extracted hashes are retained. Version 0.2.0 adds a bundled-browser executable and host-environment mode; its seven current hashes are separately recorded in runtimeRevision. Stable extractor identifiers remain distinct, and the legacy `getaudit-login.json` dedicated-profile marker. No customer data, account state or credentials are part of the project.

This is a separate local project with a private package, not a published package or public repository. The existing decision to keep the audit/research core free and open source continues to apply. No new license or public distribution has been enacted by this extraction.
